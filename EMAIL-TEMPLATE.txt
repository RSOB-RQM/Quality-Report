Subject: RSOB Quality Performance Dashboard - Now Available (Self-Service)

Hi Team,

I'm excited to share the RSOB Quality Performance Dashboard — a self-service tool where you can check your quality performance, defect rates, common errors, and best practices anytime.

HOW TO ACCESS:
==============
1. Download the attached zip file: RSOB-Quality-Dashboard.zip
2. Extract it to any folder on your desktop
3. Double-click "OPEN-DASHBOARD.bat"
4. Your browser will open automatically
5. Enter your login ID (same as your corporate login) and click Sign In

WHAT YOU'LL SEE:
================
- Your weekly & monthly defect rate trends
- Error attribute breakdown (ADM, RA, RRC, ACC, RV)
- Common defect patterns at process level & individual level
- Best practices to avoid errors
- Week-over-week comparison
- Raw audit data for your transactions

Managers will see their team's performance summary, associate-wise breakdown, and repeated defaulter tracking.

FILTERS AVAILABLE:
==================
- Region: NA / EU / Both
- Weeks: Last 4 weeks, Last 8 weeks, All, or individual week
- Month: January through April

Data is updated weekly. You'll receive the updated dashboard file each week.
