### Before vs After Initiative (Wk 8)

Pre-initiative (Wk 1–7) defect rate was **7.50%**. Post-initiative (Wk 8–16), the defect rate has come down to **5.71%**, with the most recent week (Wk 16) hitting **3.70%** — a **50% reduction** from the pre-initiative baseline and the lowest recorded in 2026.

**Note:** The current defect data includes both associate errors and tool/system-driven opportunities. I'm working with the QA team to isolate associate-only errors, which would reflect an even lower defect rate and give a more accurate picture of true associate performance.

### Top 3 defects addressed

- **Wrong Reason Code Selection (RRC)** — ~50–60% of all defects. Identified gaps in reason code selection patterns, provided input for the Hawkeye script to the POC for automated flagging. Shared best-practice flyers with the NA team covering correct SOP references.
- **Outdated RS Validator Tool Version** — Drove shift-level awareness through feedback loops. Near-zero instances in late April.
- **Incorrect BID Approvals & Missing Annotations** — With focused coaching, these errors have dropped significantly and now occur only occasionally.

### Other key actions

- Drove **100% feedback compliance** across all shifts — every defect received timely coaching.
- Shared flyers with NA team on RQM best practices and common pitfalls.

### Q1 Summary (Jan–Mar)

Q1 closed at an overall defect rate of **7.07%** across 1826 audits. January started at 7.16% as the baseline, February showed early improvement at 6.06% after the initiative launched in Week 8, and March saw a temporary spike to 8.03% driven by new RS Validator tool version rollouts and associated transition errors. Despite the March spike, the initiative laid the groundwork — feedback compliance was driven to 100%, best-practice flyers were shared with the NA team, and Hawkeye script inputs were provided to address the top defect (wrong reason code selection).

### April Summary (Wk 13–16)

April is where the initiative is showing clear results. The month started at 8.96% (Wk 13) and has steadily improved week over week — 5.26% (Wk 14), 5.26% (Wk 15), and **3.70% (Wk 16)**. April overall stands at **5.96%** across 470 audits, with the last two weeks consistently at or below the 5% target. Week 16 at 3.70% is the best performance recorded in 2026, reflecting a **50% reduction** from the pre-initiative baseline of 7.50%.
